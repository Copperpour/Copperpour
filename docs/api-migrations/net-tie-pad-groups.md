### Placement, Update PCB and `update_footprints_from_library` carry net-tie pad groups (minor release)

[#85](https://github.com/Copperpour/Copperpour/issues/85), part A:

- **Now carried by placement:** a library footprint's `(net_tie_pad_groups …)`, by `place_component`, `place_component_array`, `duplicate_component` and `add_mounting_hole` while the board is open in KiCad, and by `update_pcb_from_schematic` when it adds or replaces a part. Before, placement left the groups out and listed "footprint child 'net_tie_pad_groups' is not carried by typed placement" in `not_carried`, so a net tie reached the board as a footprint whose copper shorts its nets.
- **Now refreshed:** `update_footprints_from_library` takes such a footprint, which it refused by name ("footprint child 'net_tie_pad_groups' is not supported by typed library refresh"), and gives the placed footprint the library's groups, as KiCad's Update Footprints from Library does. A footprint whose groups differ from the library's, in their pads or their order, plans the new `changed_domains` value `net_ties`; one placed from an unchanged library plans `noop`. The apply reads the groups back from the board, and a board that does not hold them makes the result `mutation_outcome_uncertain`.
- **Older files:** a library footprint of format 20220815 or older whose keywords start with "net tie" has one group of every pad, as KiCad reads it.
- **KiCad 10.0.0:** its API stores a group's pad numbers joined with a space, which KiCad then reads as one pad, and its readback cannot show that. On 10.0.0, placement leaves the groups out and lists them in `not_carried` (Update PCB in a `not_carried` diagnostic), and the refresh and Update PCB's replacement refuse such a footprint as `unsupported_library_footprint` before writing.
- **Now refused:** a group KiCad's API would not give back as the library writes it refuses the footprint in each of these tools, naming the group, on the closed-board placement path too: a clause holding anything but quoted strings, a group that names no pad or has an empty pad number, a pad number with a backslash or whitespace in it, a group not written as its pad numbers joined with `", "` (such as `"1,2"`), a pad the footprint does not have, or a pad named twice. Before, placement listed the clause in `not_carried` and the refresh refused it by name.
- **Unchanged:** `repair_corrupted_footprints` keeps the board footprint's own groups, as it keeps the rest of its definition, and no longer lists the clause in `not_carried`. An Update PCB update of a part already on the board keeps its groups.
